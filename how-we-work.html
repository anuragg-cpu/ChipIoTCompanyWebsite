How We Work

From a first conversation to a delivered product

Three stages, one accountable engineering team throughout — no handoffs between agencies.

01

Discover

Our engineers understand customer needs, open a dialogue on the best way to fulfill electronic requirements, then hand finalized asks to the dev/engineering team.

02

Design & Prototype

Developers identify required microcontrollers, sensors, and power sources; engineers source components and build a proof of concept.

03

Validate & Deliver

The proof of concept is showcased to the client, followed by an iterative prototyping and testing cycle led by the testing team, ending in a robust prototype ready for the client's business.

STAGE 01

What happens in Discover

Requirement gathering, feasibility discussion, and scoping — turning a rough idea into a specification the engineering team can execute against.

STAGE 02

What happens in Design & Prototype

Component selection (MCUs, sensors, power), schematic and board design, and a working proof of concept built to test the core assumption.

STAGE 03

What happens in Validate & Deliver

Client review of the PoC, iterative refinement, formal testing, and a prototype that's ready to move into your business — manufacturing, field trials, or launch.
