Flagship Product

ABHAY

A sub-GHz RF wireless emergency alert system — built to raise the alarm even when the network can't. Offline-first by design, operating in the 865–867 MHz license-free band.

THE PROBLEM

When the network is down, alerts don't reach anyone

Wi-Fi and cellular-dependent alert systems fail exactly when infrastructure is stressed — during outages, in remote sites, or in buildings with poor coverage.

THE APPROACH

Sub-GHz RF, purpose-built

ABHAY communicates over a dedicated sub-GHz RF link in the 865–867 MHz band, independent of any third-party network — so it works when it's needed most.

THE RESULT

Offline-first, always ready

No internet dependency, no subscription required to function, no single point of failure shared with the building's IT infrastructure.
