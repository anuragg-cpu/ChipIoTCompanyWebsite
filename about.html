Who We Are

A trailblazer in IoT and embedded systems

ChipIOT Embedded Solutions is a trailblazer in the realm of the Internet of Things (IoT) and embedded systems. Our expertise lies in transforming cutting-edge ideas into tangible realities, helping our clients lead the way in technological innovation. Based in Pune, India, we are dedicated to advancing the field of IoT and embedded technology through our bespoke services.

A sister concern of Paras Telecom Pvt Ltd, ChipIOT was born out of the need to help vendors, dealers, and distributors connected with Paras Telecom develop their own product lines.

EST. 1996

Paras Telecom Pvt Ltd

Our parent concern, based in Aundh, Pune. Three decades in telecom and electronics distribution built a network of vendors, dealers, and distributors across the region.

SISTER CONCERN

ChipIOT Embedded Solutions Pvt Ltd

Formed to give that same network the engineering capability to design, prototype, and manufacture products under their own brand — not just distribute someone else's.
