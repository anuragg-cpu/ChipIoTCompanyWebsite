About ChipIOT

Turning cutting-edge ideas into manufacturable products

ChipIOT Embedded Solutions is a trailblazer in the realm of the Internet of Things (IoT) and embedded systems. Our expertise lies in transforming cutting-edge ideas into tangible realities, helping our clients lead the way in technological innovation. Based in Pune, India, we are dedicated to advancing the field of IoT and embedded technology through our bespoke services.

A sister concern of Paras Telecom Pvt Ltd, ChipIOT was born out of the need to help vendors, dealers, and distributors connected with Paras Telecom develop their own product lines.

How We Work

From a first conversation to a delivered product

01

Discover

Our engineers understand customer needs, open a dialogue on the best way to fulfill electronic requirements, then hand finalized asks to the dev/engineering team.

02

Design & Prototype

Developers identify required microcontrollers, sensors, and power sources; engineers source components and build a proof of concept.

03

Validate & Deliver

The PoC is showcased to the client, followed by an iterative prototyping and testing cycle, ending in a robust prototype ready for the client's business.
